# Album 127 — The Throne Is His

The Throne Is His ( சிங்காசனம் அவருடையதே)

Remnant Seats Jesus Christ — Seventy-Seven Tracks

Seventy-seven tracks declaring that the throne belongs only to the Lord Jesus Christ forever. From the first promise in Genesis, through the prophets, the full New Testament, and to Revelation. The remnant seats Him on His throne. No other. One and only living God. Tamil and English. Sense kept.
